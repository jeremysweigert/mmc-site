The Architect

Built By Someone Who Actually Runs a Club Floor.

Jeremy "Swaggle" Sweigert started performing on March 13, 1993 — the night of the Blizzard of '93 in Tower City, Pennsylvania. Over the next three decades he worked his way from Pennsylvania clubs to Lafayette, Louisiana, accumulating the kind of floor-level operational knowledge that no software company can manufacture from the outside.

Before building the AI system at Michael's Men's Club, Jeremy built swaggle.com — one of the internet's earliest video-sharing platforms, running from 2002 to 2012 before YouTube reached cultural dominance. The instinct to build tools that don't exist yet is not new.

"The same obsessive attention to craft I apply to reading a room and building a set — I applied to building the tools that run the room."

What distinguishes the MMC AI system from every commercial product in the industry is that it was designed by someone who has stood behind a DJ booth for 33 years. Every feature exists because Jeremy needed it on a real night at Michael's Men's Club — not because a product manager thought it would be a good idea.

The system is entirely self-hosted, owned outright, and continuously improved. There is no vendor. There is no support contract. It runs every night, and it gets better every week.
